How much a small-business website costs

What drives the price

Scope (pages and blocks), custom design or a template, features (requests, payment, accounts, integrations) and deadline. Ready texts and photos speed things up and lower the price.

Ballpark figures

A one-page business card site starts at $400, an ad landing page at $700, a Telegram lead bot at $300. Complex services and apps are priced after a short call.

What to check before paying

What the price includes (responsive layout, request form, analytics), how many revisions, who owns the result and what support costs after launch. Mine starts at $50 a month.
